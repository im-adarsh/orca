# Third-party IDE palette acknowledgements

The im collection adapts workbench and terminal colors from `@shikijs/themes@4.5.0`, redistributed under the included MIT license. Original themes remain the work of their authors. These adaptations are independent and are not endorsed by the original projects. Source backgrounds are preserved; structural shades, selections and text are adapted for Orca. No original application branding, proprietary theme, or syntax-highlighting engine is included.

The package MIT license is in `licenses/Shiki-Themes-MIT.txt`. Additional original project notices are retained under `licenses/popular-themes/`, including the Apache-2.0 Material Theme license. Source module hashes and REA Evidence identity are recorded in `docs/popular-theme-sources.json` (in the installable modern package: `provenance/themes.json`). The recorded raw upstream license URLs establish attribution; the exact color data is pinned by the npm tarball integrity and module SHA-256 values.
